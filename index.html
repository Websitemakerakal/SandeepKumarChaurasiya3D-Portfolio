About me

With an M.A. in Education from Lovely Professional University and a BA in Hindi, Ancient History, and Education from Allahabad University, I design learning experiences that respect both the learner and the subject. My path runs through instructional design, content writing, lesson planning, and research, with a technical grounding from ITI (COPA) and an eye for clean visual communication. Along the way I've presented at national and international conferences, published research, completed a Junior Instructional Design internship, served as Campus Ambassador for WAYSPIRE, and picked up certifications in AI tools, leadership, and workplace skills — forty certificates and counting, all shown below.
